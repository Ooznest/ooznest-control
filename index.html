Machine Calibration

Select an axis to calibrate. Each axis may use a different method to ensure maximum accuracy.

Step 1: Machine Setup

AXIS: X
1

Secure a scrap piece of MDF or Wood to your wasteboard. Ensure it's at least 120mm long.

2

Install a sharp V-Bit or engraving tool.

3

Jog to the starting position on the scrap material and Zero the Z Axis on the surface.

Ensure there is enough travel in the X direction (approx 100mm) from the current position.

Step 3: Measurement

How to Read

1. Place a standard steel ruler against the cut marks, aligning the 0 mark of the ruler with the first cut mark.

2. Look along the ruler and find where a cut mark aligns perfectly with a millimeter mark on your ruler.

"The CNC marks are 0.9mm apart. By finding where they align with the 1mm marks on your ruler, we can calculate the exact error."
