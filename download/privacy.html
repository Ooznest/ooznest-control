Ooznest Control privacy policy

Updated 20 November 2026

This policy explains how Ooznest Limited handles information in Ooznest Control for Android, desktop and the web. It supplements our Privacy & Cookie Policies, which cover our website, purchases and support services.

Who is responsible

Ooznest Limited is the data controller. Contact help@ooznest.co.uk, use our contact page, or write to The Yard, Old Crown Lane, Brentwood, Essex, CM14 5TA, United Kingdom.

Using the app and connecting to your machine

No Ooznest Control account is required. The app stores preferences, connection details, macros and your analytics choice on your device. It processes the G-code files you open and communicates with the CNC controller you select over USB or your local network. Machine operation does not require optional analytics.

Controller commands, G-code and responses travel between your device and controller. Local network connections can use unencrypted HTTP, WebSocket or TCP. Use a trusted network. Optional analytics does not upload your G-code files, filenames, macro names, complete macro contents or full controller console logs.

Optional usage analytics

Analytics is off until you choose to allow it. If you consent, the app sends usage information to PostHog's EU analytics service to help us understand feature use, connection problems and controller compatibility. We rely on your consent for this optional collection.

This can include:

  • A randomly generated installation identifier and session identifier, event timestamps, operating system, app version and whether you use the web, desktop or mobile app.
  • App opens, selected tools and viewer options, jog requests, job starts, completion and stops, job statistics, and firmware update outcomes.
  • Connection method and outcome, USB/Ethernet/Wi-Fi connection type, controller family and firmware version, and numeric controller alarm or error codes.
  • Macro command categories, such as G-code command numbers, without their coordinate values or the full macro text.
  • Your IP address as part of the network request, which PostHog can use to derive approximate location when the app opens. The web version can also report its page address without query parameters or fragments. The app does not request GPS location.

Analytics requests use HTTPS. Identifiers are pseudonymous: they distinguish installations and sessions without requiring your name or email. They are not advertising IDs. The app does not include advertising or session replay.

Your choice and deletion

You can decline analytics when asked, or turn it off later in Settings → Privacy. Turning it off stops new analytics requests, cancels pending requests and removes the installation identifier from the app's stored consent record. Turning it on again creates a new identifier. This does not automatically delete events already received.

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact help@ooznest.co.uk. Tell us that your request concerns Ooznest Control. We may need further information to identify the relevant records because app analytics is not linked to an account or email address. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes locally stored preferences and identifiers, subject to your device's backup settings.

Sharing, storage and retention

PostHog processes optional analytics for Ooznest using its EU service. We use the information for product improvement and troubleshooting, not for advertising. We do not sell app analytics. If you contact support, information you choose to provide is handled under Ooznest's main privacy policy.

We retain app information only for as long as needed for these purposes and applicable legal obligations, and consider deletion requests under applicable law. Local preferences remain until you change or clear them. You can contact us for information about retention of your records. International transfers, where applicable, are subject to the safeguards described in our main policy.

Young users

Ooznest Control is intended for users aged 13 and above. Optional analytics remains subject to consent. Young users should involve a parent or guardian where their consent is required by local law. Follow the machine's operating instructions and supervision requirements.

Your rights and policy changes

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of your personal information, withdraw consent, and complain to a supervisory authority. See our main privacy policy for further details, including the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. We will update the date above when this app policy changes. Material changes to optional analytics may require renewed consent.
